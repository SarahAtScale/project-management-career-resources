# CAPM Exam Guide

Applying, Studying, and Passing with Confidence

---

## 1. Applying for the CAPM Exam

### Eligibility (you only need **one**)

You must meet **either** of these:

* **High school diploma (or equivalent)**
  **OR**
* **23 hours of project management education** (can be an online course - I recommend https://www.udemy.com/course/capm-pmbok6/?couponCode=MT260126G2B)

You **do not** need formal project management job experience.

---

### Application Steps

1. Create an account with **Project Management Institute (PMI)**
2. Complete the CAPM application (≈15–20 minutes)
3. Pay the exam fee:
   * **PMI member:** ~$225
   * **Non-member:** ~$300
     *(Often cheaper to join PMI first)*

Once approved, you have **1 year** to take the exam. 

---

## 2. Understanding the CAPM Exam

### Exam Format

* **150 multiple-choice questions**
* **3 hours**
* **No scheduled breaks** (but you can take unscheduled ones)
* **Online (proctored)** or **in-person** at a test center

### Content Breakdown (based on PMBOK 7 + Agile)

Expect questions across:
* Project fundamentals & principles
* Predictive (Waterfall) methods
* Agile & hybrid approaches
* Business analysis basics
* Roles, artifacts, and terminology

CAPM is **terminology-heavy**, not experience-heavy.

---

## 3. Study Strategy (6–8 Week Plan)
*See r/capm on Reddit for valuable tips*

### Week 1–2: Foundations

**Goal:** Understand the landscape
* Project life cycles (predictive vs agile vs hybrid)
* Key roles (PM, sponsor, stakeholders)
* Core definitions and vocabulary

Focus on:
* PMBOK Guide (7th Edition)
* Agile concepts (Scrum, Kanban basics)

---

### Week 3–4: Knowledge Areas & Methods

**Goal:** Build pattern recognition
* Risk, schedule, cost, quality (high level)
* Change control
* Stakeholder & communication concepts
* Agile ceremonies, backlogs, increments

Tip: CAPM asks *“Which is correct?”* more than *“What would you do?”*

---

### Week 5–6: Practice Exams & Weak Spots

**Goal:** Exam readiness
* Take **2–3 full-length practice exams**
* Review *why* wrong answers are wrong
* Drill:
  * Agile vs predictive distinctions
  * Input/output artifacts
  * Situational “best fit” questions

Target: **75–80% consistently** on practice exams before sitting for the actual exam

---

## 4. How to Study Effectively (What Actually Works)

### Do This

✅ Memorize **definitions and terms**
✅ Learn **Agile vocabulary** (this is heavily tested)
✅ Practice eliminating 2 wrong answers quickly
✅ Read questions **slowly**—PMI wording is precise

### Avoid This

❌ Over-studying formulas (CAPM ≠ PMP)
❌ Assuming real-world logic beats PMI logic
❌ Cramming the night before

---

## 5. Test Day Playbook

### Before the Exam
* Sleep. Seriously.
* Eat something light.
* If remote:
  * Clean desk
  * Stable internet
  * No papers, phones, or second screens
  * Tell anyone who lives with you that they must not interrupt you.

### During the Exam
* First pass: answer confidently, flag doubts
* Second pass: review flagged questions
* Don’t overthink—**first instinct is often right**

Pace: ~1 question per minute, with buffer time at the end

---

## 6. Passing & What Comes Next

### Results
* Online exam: **Pass/fail immediately**
* Official confirmation via PMI account

If you pass, you earn the **CAPM®** designation for **3 years**.

---

### After CAPM: Smart Next Steps

Depending on your goals:
* **PMP** → once you have experience
* **PMI-ACP or Scrum certs** → if Agile-leaning
* **Program / TPM roles** → CAPM + Agile cert is a strong combo

---

## Quick Reality Check

* CAPM is **very passable** with structured study
* It rewards **discipline and familiarity**, not intuition
* Most people who fail either:
  * Skip practice exams
  * Underestimate Agile content
  * Don’t study PMI-specific wording
