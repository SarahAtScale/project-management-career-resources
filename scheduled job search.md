### Important Location Verification Rule

Do **not** rely solely on the location shown in search results, LinkedIn, or job-board summaries.

Before recommending a role:

1. Open the full job description.
2. Read the entire location and work-arrangement language.
3. Look specifically for requirements involving:
   - Hybrid work
   - Office attendance
   - Residency restrictions
   - Approved states
   - Commuting distance
   - Required proximity to an office
   - Time-zone requirements
   - Travel
   - Relocation
4. If the full job description contradicts a "remote" label, use the requirements in the full job description.

Do not recommend a role if its actual geographic or office requirements are incompatible with my location or preferences.

## Search and Screening Instructions

Search for **newly posted jobs** that meet the criteria above.

For every potentially relevant job:

1. Read the **full job description** before recommending it.
2. Verify that its location and work-arrangement requirements are compatible with my home location.
3. Compare the responsibilities and qualifications directly against my uploaded resume.
4. Determine whether I am a **realistically strong candidate**, not merely technically eligible.
5. Exclude roles that violate any stated dealbreakers or hard filters.
6. Prefer **quality over quantity**.
7. Do not recommend mediocre matches merely because they satisfy some filters.
8. Do not repeatedly recommend the same opening.
9. Prioritize newly posted roles.
10. If a posting date is available, include it.
11. When possible, link directly to the **employer's application page** instead of a job-board repost.
12. If a job has been reposted, try to determine whether it is genuinely new or simply a refreshed older posting.
13. Avoid recommending roles that appear stale, closed, or no longer actively recruiting.

## Resume Fit Analysis

For each job, evaluate my fit based on:

- Relevant TPM / program management experience
- Technical depth
- Engineering collaboration
- Program scale and complexity
- Cross-functional leadership
- Industry experience
- Platform / infrastructure experience
- AI / ML experience, when relevant
- SaaS experience
- Leadership expectations
- Required years of experience
- Required tools or methodologies
- Domain-specific knowledge
- Educational requirements
- Any explicitly required qualifications

Do not reject me simply because I lack one "preferred" qualification if my overall experience makes me a strong candidate.

However, do not recommend roles where I am missing a clearly essential requirement.
