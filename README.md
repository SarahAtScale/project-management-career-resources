# Project Management Career Resources
This repo provides resources to help you 
* Choose a project management certification
* Successfully pass the PMP (Project Management Professional) exam or successfully pass the CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management) exam
* Prep for the exam
* Update your resume with your credential and optimize it for project management roles
* Update your LinkedIn with your credential and optimize it for project management roles
* Search for and land a new role in project management
