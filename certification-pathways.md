# Project Management Certifications

## PMI - Project Management International
PMI is the most recognized licensing organization for project management. They offer a couple of project management certifications that will help you get your foot in the door and get interviews.

### CAPM - Certified Associate in Project Management
If you are an early-career project manager or are just starting out, the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) is a great option.

#### CAPM Eligibility Requirements
* Educational Background: You must have a secondary degree — such as a high school diploma, GED, associate’s degree, or a global equivalent.
* Project Management Education: You need to complete at least 23 hours of formal project management education or training before you take the exam. This education should cover project management fundamentals and can be completed through online or in-person courses, workshops, or PMI-approved training providers.

### PMP - Project Management Professional
If you have some project management experience, the Project Management Professional (PMP) certification is what you want. 
  
#### PMP Exam Eligibility Requirements
PMI offers two eligibility paths, depending on your level of formal education.

##### Path 1: With a Four-Year Degree
You must have:
* Educational Background: A four-year degree (bachelor’s degree or global equivalent)
* Project Management Experience: 36 months (3 years) of leading or directing projects within the last 8 years
* Project Management Education: 35 hours of formal project management education/training, OR An active CAPM® certification (this waives the 35-hour requirement)

##### Path 2: With a Secondary Degree
You must have:
* Educational Background: A secondary degree (high school diploma, GED, associate’s degree, or global equivalent)
* Project Management Experience: 60 months (5 years) of leading or directing projects within the last 8 years
* Project Management Education: 35 hours of formal project management education/training, OR An active CAPM® certification

##### What Counts as “Leading or Directing Projects," according to PMI?
* Managing scope, schedule, cost, resources, risks, stakeholders, or delivery
* Experience can come from any industry
* Experience does not need the job title “Project Manager”
* Overlapping projects cannot be double-counted

#### Important Notes
* PMI audits applications randomly, so your experience and education should be accurate and verifiable
* PMI membership is not required, but members receive a discounted exam fee
* Once your application is approved, you have one year to take the exam (up to 3 attempts)
* The PMP exam is process- and scenario-based, not memorization-heavy

## Google Project Management Certificate
Google also offers a Project Management Certificate, but it has not yet caught up with the PMP as the industry standard for project management certification. There are many job ads that require the PMP, but few/none that specifically look for the Google Project Management certificate.

## Additional Project Management Certifications

### Agile & Scrum Certifications
Best if you work in product, engineering, or fast-moving teams.

### Scrum Alliance
* Certified ScrumMaster (CSM) – Scrum fundamentals + facilitation
* Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) – Backlog ownership, stakeholder alignment
* Advanced CSM (A-CSM) / Certified Scrum Professional (CSP) – Senior Agile leadership
(From Scrum Alliance)

### Scrum.org
* Professional Scrum Master (PSM I / II / III) – Exam-only, more rigorous than CSM
* Professional Scrum Product Owner (PSPO)
(From Scrum.org)

### PMI (Agile Track)
* PMI-ACP (Agile Certified Practitioner) – Broad Agile across Scrum, Kanban, Lean

### Scaled Agile & Enterprise Delivery
For large orgs, multiple teams, or platform work.

* SAFe Agilist (SA) – Scaled Agile Framework basics
* SAFe Program Consultant (SPC) – Train & coach SAFe across orgs
(From Scaled Agile)

### IT, Technical & Infrastructure Project Management
Strong signal in enterprise, cloud, and regulated environments.

* PRINCE2 Foundation / Practitioner – Process-driven, common in UK/EU
(From AXELOS)
* CompTIA Project+ – Entry-level, IT-focused PM
(From CompTIA)

### Program, Portfolio & Strategy Certifications
If you operate at the systems or exec layer.

* PgMP (Program Management Professional) – Multi-project, benefits realization
* PfMP (Portfolio Management Professional) – Strategy-to-execution alignment
(From Project Management Institute)

### Lean, Six Sigma & Process Improvement
Great for ops, transformation, and efficiency work.

* Lean Six Sigma Green Belt / Black Belt – Process optimization, metrics-driven change
(Offered by multiple accredited providers)

### Change Management (Highly Underrated)
Huge value if you deal with org change, resistance, or adoption.

* Prosci Change Management Certification – ADKAR-based org change leadership
(From Prosci)

### Quick “Which Should I Choose?” Guide

* Engineering / Web3 / Startup TPM → PMP + PMI-ACP or PSM
* Big Tech / Enterprise → PMP + SAFe or PgMP
* Product-heavy role → CSPO or PSPO
* Ops / Transformation → PMP + Lean Six Sigma
* Global / EU-based companies → PRINCE2
