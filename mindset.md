## Mindset

* You already have amazing experience and an ability to do whatever job you choose.
* Your next step is just the next step, not a forever step.
* There is an abundance of opportunities and the right one will find its way to you.
* Your experience is valuable and transferable.
* You know more than you think you do.
* You deserve to have exactly what you want.
* You have been successful in the past and you will be successful in the future. 💛
